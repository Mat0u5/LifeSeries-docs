# Final Life

## Overview

Final Life is the eighth season of the Life Series.

The main twists of Final Life are:
 - Final Life Boogeyman
 - The In-Between

## Final Life Boogeyman

The Boogeyman in final life is quite different from other seasons, however there are some similarities, such as them being able to attack anyone.

They do not get cured when they kill another player.

They are an anonymous player without a name tag, and they do not appear on the tab list.

Upon death, they "leave" the server, returning next session.

:::tip Boogeyman Skin
You can change the Boogeyman skin in `config/lifeseries/finallife/boogeyman.png`
:::

## In-Between

Upon death, you are sent to the In-Between realm.

In the In-Between, you can see into your past through windows, and after walking far enough, you meet a Watcher, giving you a choice between two Abilities.


:::tip Configurable
More info here: [In-Between Config](/config/inbetween)
:::

### Trivia

On your third death in the In-Between, Trivia Bot comes to visit you and gives you a question.

:::tip Configurable
More info here: [Final Life Trivia](/config/final-life-trivia)
:::


## Abilities

You can view your current abilities by pressing a keybind (by default "J").

Abilities get progressively more pvp-related and less passive the more deaths you're on.

## Lives
All players are given 6 lives at the beginning of the first session.

:::info Life Colors
- Dark Green = 6 lives
- Green = 5 lives
- Yellow = 4 lives (hostile towards greens)
- Orange = 3 lives (hostile towards greens)
- Red = 2 life (hostile towards yellows and greens)
- Dark Red = 1 life (hostile towards everyone)
:::

Players are not allowed to *initiate combat* unless they are hostile towards that life color (see above), unless in self defence from a player that *initiated combat* against them.

:::info Initiate Combat
Initiate Combat = causing any unlawful act, such as: attacking, griefing, stealing, trapping,...<br>
Combat stops once both parties flee or once a peace agreement is reached.
:::
